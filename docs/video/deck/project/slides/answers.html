Sometimes two agents want the same function. The ledger gives one of three answers. [→] Wait: pause until the other lands, then ask again. [→] Split: divide the work. [→] Depend: this work depends on the other's, so it pauses too. [→] A fixed rule decides, not a model. If it cannot, a person is asked.
