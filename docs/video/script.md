## Rules for the read

- Say "Intent", "changes", "relies on", "premise", "re-derive", "gate", "trunk", and "Wait, Split or Depend". Do not say "merge queue", "pull request", "PR" or "branch".
- "Premises are the early warning; the gate is the guarantee" is said once, at 2:50, and not again.
- Do not claim more than was run. The agents in the recording are scripted replays; the workload is scripted; a live model has run a handful of tasks, not all twelve at once. If asked, say so.
- Numbers are spoken as written above. If a retake uses a new recording, read them off the Scale and A/B tabs instead.

## Length

About 1,160 spoken words. At 150 words a minute that is 7:45 of speech; with the pauses in the shot list it comes to between 8:00 and 8:30. If it runs long, cut the last two sentences of the 1:45 row and the sentence about cost in the 5:00 row.
