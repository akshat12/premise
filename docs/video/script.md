**Two words, kept apart.** The *screen recording* is the video this script is for. ## Rules for the read

- Say "Intent", "changes", "relies on", "premise", "re-derive", "gate", "trunk", and "Wait, Split or Depend". Do not say "merge queue", "pull request", "PR" or "branch".
- "Premises are the early warning. The gate is the guarantee." is said once, in the live-model row.
- Say "Agent 1" to "Agent 4", as the page does. The callsigns (T01, T11) are not on screen any more.
- Do not say "race". The rebase is tried first; the agent starts only if it does not pass.
- Do not claim more than was run. The agents in the recording are scripted; the workload is scripted; the comparison's scenario is planted. A live model has run single tasks and four agents at once (twice, on Cloudflare; one regression reached trunk each time), not all twelve. With no hint and nothing to adopt, a small model wrote a correct assertion of its own in 2 of 6 runs; say so if asked.
- Do not say the three were "stopped before the gate" unless the recording shows it: an Intent that had already pushed is stopped at the gate.

## Length

About 875 spoken words. At 150 words a minute that is about 5:50 of speech; with pauses it comes to about 6:20, inside the 5 to 10 minutes the rules ask for. Do not cut it below five minutes. If it runs long, shorten the At scale row first. Do not drop the live-model row: without it the read is about 745 words, which is under five minutes, and it is where "Premises are the early warning. The gate is the guarantee." is said. It is also the part a careful judge will respect most.
