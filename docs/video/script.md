## Rules for the read

- Say "Intent", "changes", "relies on", "premise", "re-derive", "gate", "trunk", and "Wait, Split or Depend". Do not say "merge queue", "pull request", "PR" or "branch".
- "Premises are the early warning. The gate is the guarantee." is said once, at the end of the A/B row.
- Do not say "race". The rebase is tried first; the agent starts only if it does not pass.
- Do not claim more than was run. The agents in the recording are scripted; the workload is scripted; the comparison's scenario is planted. A live model has run single tasks and three agents at once, not all twelve. With no hint and nothing to adopt, a small model wrote a correct assertion of its own in 2 of 6 runs; say so if asked.
- Do not say the three were "stopped before the gate" unless the recording shows it: an Intent that had already pushed is stopped at the gate.

## Length

About 880 spoken words. At 150 words a minute that is just under 6:00 of speech; with pauses it comes to between 6:00 and 6:30. If it runs long, cut the 3:20 row (the ledger) first, then the last sentence of the 2:55 row.
